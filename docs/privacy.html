Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 November 2026

RainPulse ("the app") is made by NeuralOrbitX. We built it to collect as little as possible. We have no user accounts, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking, and we do not run servers that store your data.

What stays on your iPhone

  • Your saved places, settings and alert choices are stored only on your device (and its backups).
  • Recent forecasts are cached on the device for a short time so the app opens quickly.
  • AI insights are generated on your iPhone by Apple's on-device model.
  • You can erase all of this at any time in Settings → Privacy & Security.

Location

If you allow it, the app uses your location to show weather where you are and to send rain alerts. Your location is used to request weather data from the services below. For third-party services we round coordinates to about 1 km. We never sell, share or store your location ourselves.

Weather data services

To show weather, the app sends approximate coordinates or a map area (never your name, contacts or identifiers) to:

  • Apple WeatherKit: forecasts. Governed by Apple's privacy policy; Apple does not link requests to your Apple Account.
  • Open-Meteo: rain nowcast, extended forecast, air quality, the four-model forecast confidence check, and recorded rainfall used to score past forecasts.
  • NDMA SACHET (Government of India): official weather warnings for India. The app downloads the national warning list; your location is not sent. Matching to your district happens on your phone.
  • OpenWeather: rain map images for the visible map area.
  • NOAA GFS via PacIOOS (University of Hawaiʻi): wind and rain forecast data for the visible map area.
  • Apple Weather also supplies official weather alerts outside India, where available.
  • GDACS (European Commission / United Nations): global list of active tropical cyclones (no location sent).

These providers receive your device's IP address as part of any internet request, as with any website.

Purchases

Subscriptions are handled by Apple. We never see your payment details. The app checks Apple's signed receipt on your device to unlock features.

Notifications

Rain and cyclone alerts are created on your device. We do not use push notification servers.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Your rights

Because we hold no personal data, there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete on our side. Everything the app stores can be deleted in the app or by deleting the app.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.
