Common questions

Why does the map say "Rain forecast (model)"?
Live radar shows rain right now. Future frames come from NOAA's GFS weather model, which shows where rain systems are heading in less detail than radar.

I don't get rain alerts.
Open Settings → Alerts in RainPulse, turn alerts on, and allow notifications and location ("Always" or "While Using") in iPhone Settings → RainPulse.

How do I restore my subscription?
Open Settings in RainPulse → Subscription → Restore purchases. Subscriptions belong to your Apple Account.

How do I cancel?
iPhone Settings → your name → Subscriptions → RainPulse. You keep access until the end of the paid period.

How do I delete my data?
Settings → Privacy & Security → Erase all my RainPulse data. We keep no data on our servers.
