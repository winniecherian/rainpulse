    Common questions

    Why does the map say "Rain forecast (model)"?
    The rain map shows OpenWeather's estimate of rain right now. Future frames come from NOAA's GFS weather model, which shows where rain systems are heading in less detail than radar.

    Where do official warnings come from?
    In India, from the government's NDMA SACHET system, which carries warnings from IMD, CWC, INCOIS and state disaster authorities. Elsewhere, from Apple Weather's official alerts where available. They are shown in the agency's own words and are free on every plan. Serious (severe or extreme) warnings also send a notification; turn this off in Settings → Alerts → Official warnings.

    What is "Forecast confidence"?
    RainPulse asks four independent weather models (ECMWF, GFS, ICON and GEM) whether it will rain in the next 12 hours, and when. If they agree, confidence is high; if they disagree, the app says so.

    What is "Forecast vs reality"?
    RainPulse saves each 3-hour rain forecast and later checks it against Rain Truth reports from people nearby, or, when nobody reported, against Open-Meteo's recorded rainfall (a model estimate, not a rain gauge). The score shows how often the forecast was right at that place over the last 7 days.

    I don't get rain alerts.
    Open Settings → Alerts in RainPulse, turn alerts on, and allow notifications and location ("Always" or "While Using") in iPhone Settings → RainPulse.

    How do I restore my subscription?
    Open Settings in RainPulse → Subscription → Restore purchases. Subscriptions belong to your Apple Account.

    How do I cancel?
    iPhone Settings → your name → Subscriptions → RainPulse. You keep access until the end of the paid period.

    How do I delete my data?
    Settings → Privacy & Security → Erase all my RainPulse data. We keep no data on our servers.
